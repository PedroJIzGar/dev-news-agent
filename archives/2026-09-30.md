**Score:** 9/10  
**Fuente:** The Hacker News  
**Categoría:** Seguridad  
**URL:** https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/new-spectre-v2-btr-attack-leaks-linux.html

**URL:** https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/french-tax-data-theft-using-stolen.html
